# Senior HR Coordinator — SOP & SLA Reference Framework

> Reference guidance only. Confirm statutory deadlines with Legal for every state and locality in which you employ people.

## 1. Role scope

A Senior HR Coordinator owns the operational backbone of People Operations: data integrity, onboarding and offboarding execution, leave administration, compliance recordkeeping and first-line employee relations intake. The role differs from a junior coordinator by owning outcomes, audits and escalations rather than transactions alone.

| Area | Ownership |
| --- | --- |
| HRIS and data | System-of-record accuracy, ATS/HRIS integration checks, audit design |
| Lifecycle | Pre-boarding through offboarding, including cross-functional coordination |
| Compliance | I-9, leave, retention, postings, EEO/OFCCP data, benefits notices |
| Employee relations | Intake, triage, documentation and escalation (not investigation decisions) |
| Team | Mentoring junior coordinators, QA sampling, SOP maintenance |

## 2. Service-level agreements

| Process | Target SLA | Escalate if |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tier-2 HRIS ticket first response | 4 business hours | Not triaged by the 3-hour mark |
| Offer letter turnaround | < 24 hours from approved request | Comp approval missing at hour 12 |
| Background check status review | Daily | Result pending beyond vendor SLA |
| I-9 Section 1 | By the first day of work | Not complete at end of Day 1 |
| I-9 Section 2 | Within 3 business days of start | Open at start of business day 3 |
| FMLA eligibility notice | 5 business days from notice of need | Day 3 with no notice drafted |
| FMLA designation notice | 5 business days after sufficient information | Day 3 |
| Benefits enrollment (new hire) | 30 days from start | Day 25 with no election |
| ER concern acknowledgement | 1 business day | Same-day for safety or executive concerns |
| Final pay | State deadline (may be same day) | Calculation not handed off 3 days before separation |
| COBRA qualifying-event notice to administrator | Within 30 days | Day 14 |
| UI claim response | State deadline | 2 days before the deadline |

## 3. Standard cadences

### Daily
1. HRIS inbox and ticket triage.
2. Background check and I-9 aging review.
3. Leave intake log.
4. Timecard exceptions before payroll cutoff.
5. Next-five-days start readiness check.

### Weekly
1. Cross-functional onboarding sync.
2. ATS-to-HRIS sync audit.
3. Offer letter and compensation band QA.
4. People Ops digest to HRBPs.
5. Separation pipeline review.

### Monthly
1. Pre-payroll audit.
2. Benefits reconciliation.
3. Leave tracker reconciliation.
4. Work-authorization expiry report.
5. File sampling (10 files).

### Quarterly and annual
- Quarterly: HRIS data integrity and EEO-1/OFCCP audit, access review, training completion, policy review.
- Bi-annual: performance review and calibration logistics.
- Annual: open enrollment, ACA 1095-C, postings, compensation cycle, records retention review, OSHA 300A posting (Feb 1 – Apr 30).

## 4. Offer letter QA checklist
- Salary within the approved band; level and title match the requisition.
- Exempt/non-exempt classification validated (salary basis, salary level, duties).
- Equity grant matches the approved grant table and states vesting.
- Sign-on bonus includes a state-compliant repayment provision.
- Pay transparency requirements met for the work location.
- Contingencies (background check, I-9, agreements) clearly stated.
- At-will language included where appropriate; no promise of employment term.
- Start date, manager and location correct; acceptance deadline stated.

## 7. Employee relations intake rules
1. Listen, take notes in the employee's words, do not offer conclusions.
2. Do not promise absolute confidentiality.
3. Remind everyone involved about anti-retaliation obligations.
4. Escalate immediately: harassment or discrimination by leaders, threats, self-harm, whistleblower or wage claims, legal demands, accommodation denials, media interest, union activity.
5. Keep the investigation file separate from the personnel file.

## 8. Records retention baseline (federal)

| Record | Minimum |
| --- | --- |
| I-9 | 3 years after hire or 1 year after termination, whichever is later |
| FLSA payroll records | 3 years; time cards 2 years |
| EEOC personnel and hiring records | 1 year |
| ADEA payroll records | 3 years |
| FMLA | 3 years |
| OSHA 300/300A/301 | 5 years |
| ERISA plan records | 6 years |

State laws and litigation holds may extend these periods. Many employers adopt a 7-year default for risk management.

## 9. Control and audit checkpoints
- **Segregation:** I-9, medical and investigation records are kept separate from personnel files.
- **Least privilege:** Review HRIS roles quarterly.
- **Dual control:** Compensation changes require approval and an independent data-entry check.
- **Evidence:** Retain screenshots, approvals and timestamps for every audit.
- **Root cause:** Every error type in a QA sample gets a corrective action and an owner.

## 10. Maturity path: Junior → Senior
1. Executes checklists accurately.
2. Spots patterns and fixes root causes.
3. Designs audits and automations.
4. Owns Tier-2 escalations and stakeholder syncs.
5. Mentors others and presents metrics with recommendations.
